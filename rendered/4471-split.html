Each report is placed by its tenant's locale and booking form (causes.ts), not by the tag intake guessed from the reporter's browser. The two disagree on some reports, so a count by intake tag is wrong. The export repeats some reports; the table counts each id once.

The two problems

  1. parser: dates are read month first. The shared parser reads every date month first. Ticket: issue 1. Fix: read dates by the tenant's locale.
  2. hint: the booking-v3 form hint is month first. The form prints a month-first hint under its date field. People at day-first tenants type what the hint says, and the form reads it day first. Ticket: issue 2. Fix: change the hint the form prints. A parser fix does not touch it.

Lessons

  • Merging on the subject line joined reports whose causes differ. Count the cause, not the subject.
  • A tag guessed at intake is a guess. The tenant says which cause a report has.
  • A number that can be computed is computed: the table above is built from the data.
